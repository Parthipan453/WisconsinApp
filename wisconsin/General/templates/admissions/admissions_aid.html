Our promise to Wisconsin students

UW–Madison is within your reach. That’s our promise to all Wisconsin students, no matter their financial means. Since 2017, we’ve launched a series of tuition-free initiatives that have made the UW a leader in affordability while transforming thousands of students’ lives. As a result, nearly two-thirds of our students leave UW–Madison debt-free.

Bucky's Tuition Promise

Free tuition for incoming Wisconsin resident students whose family's household adjusted gross income is $65,000 or less.

Program details

Bucky’s Pell Pathway

A commitment to meet the full financial need of incoming Wisconsin resident students who are eligible for the Federal Pell Grant.

Program details

Wisconsin Tribal Educational Promise

A program to fund the full cost of attendance for Wisconsin resident students who are enrolled members of a federally recognized American Indian tribe.

Program details for Undergraduates

Badger Promise

Free tuition for first-generation college students who are Wisconsin residents and have transferred from a two-year UW Branch campus or equivalent program.

Annual Security and Fire Safety Report

The Annual Security and Fire Safety Report contains current campus safety and disciplinary policies, crime statistics, and on-campus student housing fire safety policies and fire statistics for the previous three calendar years. UW–Madison will provide a paper copy upon request: contact the University of Wisconsin Police Department at uwpolice@mhub.uwpd.wisc.edu.

Voluntary System of Accountability

UW–Madison participates in the Voluntary System of Accountability (VSA), a program designed to provide accountability through accessible, transparent, and comparable information for more than 260 public colleges and universities.
