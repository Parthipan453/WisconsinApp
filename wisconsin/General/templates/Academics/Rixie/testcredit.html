International Baccalaureate (IB)
Cambridge A-Levels

For specific information about what transfer credit will be awarded and the scores required to earn that credit, refer to the Guide using the links below.

STEP 2
Review your credit
evaluation in MyUW
›

Log into your MyUW portal to view your test credit evaluation.

View Your Evaluation

Your evaluation will show which test credits have been awarded and how they apply to your degree requirements.

Processing Time

Allow 2-4 weeks for test credit evaluation after we receive your official score reports from the testing agency.

Follow the instructions provided on the official test score page. This information pertains to both applicants and currently enrolled students sending test scores.

If you have questions about your test credit evaluation, contact the Office of Admissions and Recruitment for assistance.

STEP 3
Meet with your
academic advisor
›

Schedule a meeting with your academic advisor to discuss how your test credits apply to your degree requirements.

Plan Your Degree

Your advisor will help you understand how your test credits fulfill major, general education, and elective requirements.

SOAR Orientation

Admitted students will meet with an advisor during SOAR to discuss test credits and course registration.

Bring your test credit evaluation and degree audit to your advising appointment for review.

Test credits may apply differently to various majors. Discuss your specific program requirements with your advisor.

STEP 4
Request reevaluation
(if needed)
›

If you believe a test credit was evaluated incorrectly, you may request a reevaluation with supporting documentation.

Submit a Reevaluation Request

Complete the reevaluation form and provide any supporting documentation from the testing agency.

Review Process

Academic departments review reevaluation requests and make final determinations on test credit equivalencies.

Reevaluation requests must be submitted within one semester of your initial test credit evaluation.

Not all test credits are eligible for reevaluation. Check with the academic department for specific requirements.

FAQs

|

The test credit evaluation process typically takes 2-4 weeks after we receive your official score reports. During peak times (such as the start of a semester), processing times may be longer. You can check the status of your evaluation in your MyUW portal.

UW-Madison accepts Advanced Placement (AP), International Baccalaureate (IB), Cambridge A-Levels, and College-Level Examination Program (CLEP) scores. Each test has specific score requirements for credit, which can be found in the Guide.

You must request official score reports directly from the testing agency. UW-Madison's school code for AP is 1846. For IB, Cambridge, and CLEP, please refer to the testing agency's website for instructions on sending scores to UW-Madison.

Yes, you can send test scores to UW-Madison after you enroll. However, test credits are typically only awarded if the test was taken prior to enrolling at UW-Madison. Contact the Office of Admissions and Recruitment for specific guidance.

If you disagree with your test credit evaluation, you can submit a reevaluation request. You will need to provide supporting documentation from the testing agency. The reevaluation request should be submitted within one semester of receiving your initial test credit evaluation.
