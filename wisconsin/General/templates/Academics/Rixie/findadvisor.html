Helpful Info for Scheduling Advising Appointments

Scheduling an advising appointment in Starfish

Once you log in to Starfish you will see the "My Success Network" page where you'll find a list of your assigned advisors.

1

Click on the person you'd like to schedule with.

2

Select an available appointment time.

3

Confirm your appointment and save the details.

Note: If you see a message that says there are no appointments available, try looking for a later date in the advisor's schedule. If you continue having issues, please contact the advisor or the advising office directly.
