About Majors & Certificates

What is a major and how do I complete one?

A major is a structured set of courses and requirements that allow you to develop deep knowledge in a specific field. Most students explore different options before deciding, and advisors are here to help you find the right fit. Choosing a major helps guide your academic journey and can shape future opportunities, but it doesn’t lock you into a single career path.

The requirements to complete a major vary, but overall you need a minimum of 120 course credits to graduate with a bachelor’s degree from UW-Madison. A typical major requires about 30 to 40 credits in a specific area of study.

What is a major and how do I complete one?

A major is a structured set of courses and requirements that helps you develop deep knowledge in a specific field. Exploring different options before deciding is normal, and academic advisors are available to help you find the right fit.

Most students complete at least 120 credits for a bachelor’s degree. A typical major requires around 30 to 40 credits in a particular area of study; certificates let you build additional expertise outside your major.

Declaring a Major

Declaring a major is a big decision! Some students know their intended major right away, and others take some time to figure it out—both are totally normal. The process and timeline of declaring a major at UW-Madison looks different depending on the program and major you choose.

Explore. Learn. Decide.

We're here to help step of the way.

How to declare a major

The steps to declare depend on the major you choose. You might need to apply and be admitted to the school or college that offers it, or to the major itself. Some majors require you to meet with an academic advisor first, complete specific courses, or submit a formal application or declaration form. Each major has its own process, so be sure to check the "How to Get In" section of each major's page in Guide.

Since requirements can vary, meet with your academic advisor to ensure you have the information you need!
