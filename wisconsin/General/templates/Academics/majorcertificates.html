Undergraduate majors and certificates

The University of Wisconsin–Madison offers hundreds of undergraduate majors and certificates. A major is a specialized area of study that includes a set of defined core classes and electives ranging from introductory to advanced coursework. Similar to a “minor” at other universities, a certificate offers students the ability to develop additionalexpertise in an area of study outside of a major. As you consider your career goals, find a program that’s right for you.
