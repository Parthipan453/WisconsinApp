Quick Tips

• Students must be active and enrolled in the program to be eligible for bulk enrollment.
• Filter by admission year to narrow down students by their cohort.
• Select students first, then click "Assign to Section" on the course card to finalize enrollment.
• Use the search bar to quickly find students by name or student ID.
• Students already enrolled in a course will show as "Already enrolled" and cannot be selected.
• You can assign different groups of students to different sections — selection is tracked per course.
