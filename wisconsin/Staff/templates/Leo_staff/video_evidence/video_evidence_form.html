FAQ

Dynamic Target Rules

Why is the target date not editable?

The date comes directly from the selected academic workflow record, so staff cannot enter a conflicting date.

Why does an exam not appear?

The examination must be completed and all required faculty evaluations must be submitted before it becomes eligible for video evidence.

What if the student has no exam?

The target selector reports that no examination has been created instead of showing unrelated records.

Can an existing video be updated?

Yes. Update mode keeps the same workflow rules and optionally allows the video file to be replaced.
