Graduation FAQ

Who approves a graduation record?

The graduation record is submitted to the assigned Chair Faculty for approval.

What should be checked before creation?

Verify academic progression, dissertation completion, and successful defense.

Can graduation records be reviewed later?

Yes. Staff can open an individual graduation record from the graduation cards.
