Frequently Asked Questions

Your questions are our answers. Contact us.
Issues logging into your account?+

For issues or questions related to your account, call 800-442-6459 or email contact@go.wisconsin.edu.

How can I get help with my Universities of Wisconsin Application?+

For assistance with the Universities of Wisconsin Admission Pathway Portal, call 800-442-6459 or email contact@go.wisconsin.edu.

Is there an application fee for undergraduate applications?+

For first-year and transfer applicants, the following UW universities charge an application fee for each application submitted: UW-Eau Claire ($25), UW-La Crosse ($25), and UW-Madison ($80). All other UW universities do not charge an application fee.

How can I make changes to my submitted application?+

If you have already submitted your application, contact the admissions office directly to update your information. Contact information is available in your Next Steps PDF.

What is the status of my application?+

Please contact the admissions office directly to request an update on your application status. Contact information is available in your Next Steps PDF.

What classes are available?+

Check out each campus's schedule of classes or undergraduate catalog to find the perfect class for you.
To view how credits may transfer to a UW, use the Transferology tool or contact your Academic Advisor.
