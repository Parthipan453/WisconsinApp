FAQ

Workflow Questions

Why is my evaluation not available?

Committee evaluation becomes available only after the Advisor approves the current dissertation submission.

Why is the Chair still waiting?

The Chair can finalize only after all required Co-Chair, Internal, and External committee evaluations are submitted.

What happens if a member rejects?

A committee member rejection is recorded as part of the evaluation. The Chair remains the final decision maker.

What happens after Chair revision?

The student can submit a new dissertation version, after which the Advisor, Committee, and Chair workflow starts again for the new version.
