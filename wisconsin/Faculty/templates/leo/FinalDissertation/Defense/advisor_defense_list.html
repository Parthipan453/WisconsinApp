Need Help?

Frequently Asked Questions

When does a student appear here?

The latest final dissertation submission must be approved, the doctoral committee must be approved, and the student must remain active under your supervision.

What does 0/2 submitted mean?

It means two committee evaluations are required and none have been submitted yet.

When is a defense completed?

A defense is marked completed after a final PASS or FAIL result is recorded.

Can I schedule the defense here?

Advisors can monitor the defense record. Defense scheduling is handled through the authorized scheduling workflow.
