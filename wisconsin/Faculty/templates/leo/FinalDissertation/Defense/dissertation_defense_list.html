QUICK HELP

Defense FAQ

When can a defense be scheduled?

A defense can be scheduled after the final dissertation has been approved and the doctoral committee is approved.

Who can schedule the defense?

The assigned Chair Faculty is responsible for creating and managing the dissertation defense schedule.

When are evaluations available?

Committee evaluation activity becomes available according to the defense workflow and scheduled date.

What does a completed defense mean?

A completed defense indicates that a final defense result has been recorded for the candidate.
