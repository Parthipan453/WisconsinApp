Citation Managers

Citation managers are software tools that help you collect, organize, cite and share references. They take your collection of PDFs, source citations and other references and organize them into a personal library.

UW-Madison Libraries support three main citation managers:

EndNote

EndNote is available as EndNote Basic, a free browser-based tool, or as EndNote Desktop. EndNote offers discounted pricing to students.

Mendeley

Mendeley is a free citation manager which can be used in a browser or installed on your computer. Users can opt to pay for additional cloud storage.

Zotero

Zotero is a free, open-source citation manager that runs primarily as an installed application on your computer.

Workshops

Librarians teach hands-on classes about citation managers at locations around campus.

Consultations

Library staff are happy to assist you in determining the best citation manager for your information needs or in answering any questions you may have. Please contact your liaison librarian or a member of the group of citation managers consultants.
