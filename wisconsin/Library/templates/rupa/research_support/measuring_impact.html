Measuring & Maximizing Impact

Researchers across disciplines are increasingly being asked to demonstrate the impact of their work, whether it be for promotion and tenure, grants, or accreditation. Raising the visibility of a researcher's work through curated online profiles, open access publishing, public engagement, and research metrics can significantly increase the influence of scholarly work. Our library provides expert guidance, consultation, reporting services, and workshops to help researchers maximize the visibility and impact of their scholarship.

Custom Impact Reporting & Citation Data Analysis

Campus Libraries assist research teams, departments, and centers in understanding the impact of their publications. Services include citation analysis, bibliographic reports, grant acknowledgement analysis, collaboration mapping, benchmarking reports, and customized research impact studies.

Researcher Profiles

Researcher profiles connect scholars with their publications, grants, affiliations, and professional achievements. We assist faculty and students in creating, managing, and optimizing profiles such as ORCID, Google Scholar, Scopus Author ID, and Web of Science Researcher Profiles.

Open Access Publishing

Open Access increases the visibility, accessibility, and citation potential of research outputs. Learn about institutional repositories, publishing agreements, copyright, Creative Commons licenses, and funding support for open access publication.

Altmetrics & Research Visibility

Beyond citations, alternative metrics measure the online attention received by research through news outlets, blogs, policy documents, social media, and online reference managers.

Citation Management

Learn to organize references using EndNote, Zotero, and Mendeley. Library experts provide workshops, one-to-one consultations, and citation style assistance.

Contact Us

We are continuing to offer a suite of workshops on research metrics, scholarly publishing, and more. Librarians are also available for individual or group consults tailored to your needs.
