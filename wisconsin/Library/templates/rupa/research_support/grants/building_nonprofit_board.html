Recommended Books for Nonprofit Management

The following resources are available at the Grants Information Collection (GIC) in Room 262 D/F of Memorial Library at the University of Wisconsin-Madison. When included, call numbers indicate the shelf location of the item in the GIC.

Branding for Nonprofits: Developing Identity with Integrity by DK Holland Call Number: GIC: HD69 B7 H645 2006
Complete Guide to Fundraising Management by Stanley Weinstein Call Number: GIC: HV41.9 U5 W46 2009
Don't Forget the Pizza: and Other Rules for Managing a Nonprofit: Using Business Management Tools to Improve the Effectiveness of Nonprofit Agencies by Linda M. Zukowski Call Number: GIC: HD62.6 Z85 2006
Exposing the Elephants: Creating Exceptional Nonprofits by Pamela J. Wilcox Call Number: GIC: HD62.6 W55 2006 ISBN: 0471792217
Forces for Good: The Six Practices of High-Impact Nonprofits by Leslie R. Crutchfield and Heather McLeod Grant Call Number: GIC: HD62.6 C78 2008
Grant Management: Funding for Public and Nonprofit Programs by Jeremy L. Hall Call Number: GIC: HG177 H355 2010
Human Resources Management for Public and Nonprofit Organizations: A Strategic Approach by Joan E. Pynes Call Number: GIC: HF5549.2 U5 P96 2009
