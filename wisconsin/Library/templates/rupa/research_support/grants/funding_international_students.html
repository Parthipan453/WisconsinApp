Funding for International Education: Books

The following resources are available at the Grants Information Collection (GIC) in Room 262 D/E of Memorial Library at the University of Wisconsin-Madison. When included, call numbers indicate the shelf location of the item in the GIC.

Awards, Honors, and Prizes Volume 2: International and Foreign This volume is organized by home country. It includes prizes and awards for both individuals and organizations.
Funding for United States Study: A Guide for International Students and Professionals Those already studying in the U.S. may find the "Foundations" section more helpful than the one on "Institutions."
Funding U.S. Studies: Graduate and Post-Graduate Opportunities for Latin Americans Call Number: GIC: LB2338 F95 2003
Foundation Grants To Individuals by Foundation Center Call Number: GIC: HV97 A3 F57
How to Write a Winning Scholarship Essay: 30 Essays That Won Over $3 Million in Scholarships by Gen and Kelly Tanabe Call Number: GIC: LB2338 T36 2006
Kaplan Scholarships: Billions of Dollars in Free Money for College Call Number: GIC: LB2338 S347 Includes a "Residency" index.
Scholarships, Fellowships And Loans: A Guide to Education-related Financial Aid Programs for Students and Professionals Includes a helpful "Legal Residence Index" that covers Canada in-depth, as well as some entries for other countries across the globe.
