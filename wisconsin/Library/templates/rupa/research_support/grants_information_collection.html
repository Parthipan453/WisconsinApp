Grants Information Collection

General Information and Resources

Welcome to the Grants Information Collection (GIC), a Funding Information Network partner of Candid (formerly the Foundation Center). The GIC is a library collection located in Madison, Wisconsin, where visitors conduct their own funding research.

We do not give out or manage grants.

Grants Research

  • Onsite research
    • For non-UW–Madison affiliates: We currently have at least one public workstation that does not require a login. Other workstations require credentials.
    • If you do not have a UW–Madison NetID, please contact Circulation Support to request a Guest NetID before visiting.
    • Learn more about accessing Memorial Library and the Grants Information Collection.
  • Trying to search for grants from home?
    • Register to use the Foundation Directory Online Quick Start.
    • UW–Madison affiliates may also use Pivot and other subscription funding databases.
    • Visit the Help & Tutorial page for guides and tutorials.
  • Nonprofits seeking relief funding may browse curated funding opportunities and news provided through trusted grant resources.
