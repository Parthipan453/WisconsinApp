Services at Steenbock Patent & Trademark Services

Patent & Trademark Services

Steenbock Library is a designated Patent and Trademark Resource Center (PTRC). Patent librarians provide patent and trademark information and searching assistance for the general public and the UW–Madison community.

The Patents and Trademarks Research Guide provides basic information on patents and trademarks, searching tips and tutorials, online resources, and help for inventors and researchers. For a more detailed introduction to intellectual property and patents, you can also review the following UW Libraries micro-courses:

  • Introduction to Intellectual Property
  • Introduction to Patents

Patent and Trademark Consultations

Steenbock offers patent and trademark assistance by phone (608-262-3007), email, virtual consultation, or in person:

  • Help with patent and trademark searching
  • Consult on the most useful databases and other online resources
  • Find general information on patents and trademarks, and the application/registration process
  • Referral to business, legal and entrepreneurial resources
  • Help with historical research on older items
  • Patent and trademark statistical information

Appointments strongly recommended to ensure librarian availability.

Other Patent & Trademark Resource Centers in the Region

  • Hennepin County Library (Minneapolis)
  • Chicago Public Library
  • Milwaukee Public Library

Reminder

Steenbock librarians cannot do a patent search for you or offer legal advice. We urge you to contact a registered patent attorney for legal questions about your individual situation. See our Patents Research Guide for legal resources. The completeness and accuracy of a patent or trademark search rests entirely with the individual conducting the search.
