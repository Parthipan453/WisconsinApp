Public Access

Funder public access requirements are related but distinct from open access.

Some funding agencies require that published research results enabled by their grants be made available to the general public, usually on the internet. See this Funder Public Access requirements page for more information and resources.

Public Access

  • Refers specifically to funder mandates to make research outputs accessible to the public.
  • Research outputs must be placed in a specific repository.

Open Access (OA)

  • Publications are freely available online and generally carry fewer copyright and licensing restrictions than traditionally published works.
  • Publishing in an OA journal does not necessarily satisfy a funder's public access policy.

Neither complying with funder public access requirements nor publishing open access necessarily requires authors to pay a fee.

  • Gold Open Access publications (where the version of record is freely available on the publisher's website) often require article processing charges, although not always.
  • Many publishers allow authors to deposit the accepted manuscript in an institutional or subject repository. This supports Green Open Access while also helping researchers comply with funder public access policies.
