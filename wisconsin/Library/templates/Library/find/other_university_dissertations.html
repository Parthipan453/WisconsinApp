Other University Dissertations

There are several databases for identifying dissertations and theses from other universities.

Database Years Formats
Center for Research Libraries Doctoral Dissertations 1890+ no abstracts; no full text; does not include U.S. or Canada
DART (Digital Archive of Research Theses)-Europe 1597+ over 380,000 dissertations from over 500 European universities
Directory of History Dissertations (American Historical Society) 1882+ no abstracts; no full text; dissertations completed or in progress in Canada or the United States
Dissertations of China 1986+ abstracts; some full text
EBSCO Open Dissertations 1933+ abstracts; some have links to external full-text
Index to Theses in Great Britain and Ireland 1716+ abstracts; some full text
OATD (Open Access Theses and Dissertations)   Over 16 million theses and dissertations from over 800 colleges, universities and research institutions
ProQuest Dissertations & Theses (now includes ProQuest Dissertations and Theses – UK & Ireland) 1637+ abstracts 1980+; full text PDFs 1997+ for participating institutions; 24-page preview 1997+ for most dissertations
Theses Canada Portal 1965+ no abstracts; some full text
