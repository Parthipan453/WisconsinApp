Appeal of Library Charges

Fines are created when you return items past the due date or when you lose or damage an item. An appeal is a request to have the total amount of fines reduced.

All students, staff, and faculty are eligible for a one-time appeal of overdue fines up to $50. After that, the Libraries grant appeals only for extenuating circumstances.

What Fines Can Be Appealed?

Fine Type What is it for? Can I Appeal? How Can I Resolve It?
Overdue Fine Overdue fines for course reserve materials. Yes Fill out the appeal form below.
Lost Item Replacement Fee or Damaged Item Fee Charges for lost or damaged library materials. No Return the item, pay the fine, or contact usregarding an approved replacement copy.

What is the Appeal Process?

  1. Complete the Appeal of Library Charges form below.
  2. Circulation Support staff will review your account and determine how much of the overdue fine can be waived.
  3. You will receive an email with the results of your appeal request.
  4. Pay any remaining balance on your library account.
