Streaming Video Database Information for Courses

The Libraries license a substantial amount of streaming video content that can be utilized in courses. The licensing models for each of these databases can vary widely. Below you will find information on our largest streaming video databases including which databases accept individual film requests and public performance rights information.

Individual films can be found searching the library catalog. A full list of streaming video databases can be found in our database library.

If you cannot find the content you are looking for please submit a request.

Kanopy

Streaming video service of documentaries and film across diverse subjects and disciplines. Access to Criterion Collection, Media Education Foundation Collection and California Newsreel Collection.

  • Individual film requests: Yes, we try to acquire the title when possible.
  • Public Performance Rights: Yes. Films with PPR include public performance rights as long as the film is being shown on campus and admission or fees are not charged.
  • Expiration dates: We typically license individual films for a one-year increment with the option to renew as long as Kanopy is distributing the film. Some Kanopy content is part of a package of films.

Swank Digital Campus

Streaming video collection of feature, commercial and independent films and documentaries.

  • Individual film requests: Yes, but only on a semester basis. We will make every effort to add films that are not part of our subscription package. However, we need at least 15 film requests before we can license individual titles.
  • To access the full Swank Digital Campus catalog you will need to create an instructor account.
  • Please submit requests directly to us and not in the Swank platform.
  • Please make all requests to submit requests for individual films before the start of each semester. We can only guarantee additions at this time.
  • Stream titles expire at the end of each negotiated license and will need to be renewed.
  • Public Performance Rights: No. Swank content must be viewed only for courses. This film can be streamed in the classroom or assigned for asynchronous viewing by your enrolled students.
  • Using Swank titles for purposes other than instruction, such as a public showing or outside of course work, requires full campus licensing.
  • Expiration dates: Films will expire after the license period. Renewal is based on availability.
  • Other information: Swank reserves the right to request more information about the course using a title. Library staff will connect you with Swank if we need more information to submit your request.

Academic Video Online

Collection of almost 70,000 streaming videos spanning a wide range of subject areas including anthropology, business, counselling, film, health, history, music, and more.

  • Individual film requests: No.
  • Public Performance Rights: Yes, as long as the film is being shown on campus and admission or fees are not charged. *Excludes National Theatre titles.
  • Expiration dates: Academic Video Online content is added and removed over time at the distributor's discretion. The Libraries subscribe to this database on a yearly basis.

Docuseek

Streaming video collection of independent, social-issue and environmental films from renowned leaders in documentary film distribution.

  • Individual film requests: Yes.
  • Public Performance Rights: Yes, as long as the film is being shown on campus and admission or fees are not charged.
  • Expiration dates: Video collections in Docuseek have been purchased and the Libraries retain perpetual access to the titles, while some individual titles are leased for 1 or 3 years.

Films on Demand: Master Academic Collection

25,000 streaming educational videos on a variety of academic areas of study provided by many producers (BBC, National Geographic, PBS, etc.).

  • Individual film requests: No.
  • Public Performance Rights: Yes, as long as the film is being shown on campus and admission or fees are not charged.
  • Expiration dates: Films on Demand are added and removed content at their discretion. The Libraries subscribe to this database in coordination with the Universities of Wisconsin.

Alexander Street Streaming Video Collections

The Libraries have access to a number of collections in Alexander Street, providing access to thousands of films on a wide variety of topics.

Some Alexander Street collections licensed through the Libraries include:

  • Black Studies in Video
  • Contemporary Global Issues in Video
  • Dance in Video
  • Ethnographic Video Online
  • Film Platform
  • LGBT Studies in Video
  • PBS Video Collection
  • Individual film requests: No.
  • Public Performance Rights: Yes, as long as the film is being shown on campus and admission or fees are not charged. *Excludes titles in the Film Platform Collection and National Theatre titles.
  • Expiration dates: While the Libraries have purchased some collections in Alexander Street and have perpetual access, other ones are subscribed to on an annual basis and may have titles move in and out of the collection.
