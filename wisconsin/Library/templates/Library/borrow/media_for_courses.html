Media for Courses

The UW Libraries support the use of media for courses through licensed databases, the digitization and streaming of short portions of media, and physical course reserves.

Requests

The Libraries can help you find options for films you would like students to view to meet specific pedagogical requirements for your courses. Please fill out a request form with the titles you intend to use and the Libraries can assist you in the best options for your media.

Getting Started

Please use this form to make a request , or contact reserves@library.wisc.edu with questions.

Using full Films or other Media in Courses

While current copyright law does not support the digitizing and streaming of full-length films or other media except in specific circumstances, there are still several ways to provide access to full films for your course.

  • UW Libraries license digital access to full-length films through a variety of databases. Films available to all students, staff and faculty of UW–Madison can be found through our library catalog.
  • Some databases offer individual film licenses while others license packages of materials.
  • Detailed information about our larger databases can be found here.
  • A complete list of audio and video databases can be found in our database library .
  • In accordance with 17 U.S.C. §110(1), the showing of a full film may be allowed in a face-to-face classroom setting when using the legally obtained physical media (unless the digital version has been licensed or legally obtained).
  • If a film is not able through any legal streaming service such as Netflix, Hulu, Disney+, etc., you may request the Libraries explore purchasing options.
  • A web search will often show options for streaming of films from major streaming platforms, sometimes free or free with ads.
  • Netflix has made a selection of documentary features and series openly available on the Netflix U.S. YouTube channel.
  • Many streaming services provide access only for personal viewing and do not allow classroom streaming.
  • The Libraries also maintain physical media in reserve for student use.
  • Students may use viewing stations and equipment available in the Libraries to access DVDs and other physical media.
  • You can attempt to obtain permission to use a digitized version directly from the copyright owner.
  • If permission is granted, the Libraries can assist with the digitization and streaming process.
  • You may also use films that are in the Public Domain.
  • For titles the Libraries do not currently own, please contact reserves@library.wisc.edu for assistance.

Using Portions or Clips of Films or Other Media in Courses

In many cases the Libraries can assist in the digitization and streaming of specific, short portions of media for classroom use. Instructors should carefully consider the amount of material needed to meet their teaching objectives.

Requirements for Digitization of Physical Media

  • The requested portion must support a specific pedagogical requirement for a UW–Madison course.
  • The item must be legally owned by the University or by the instructor.
  • The media must be on DVD, Blu-ray, or VHS tape.
  • The requested media must be available through the UW Libraries.
  • Access to streamed content will become unavailable at the end of the semester.
  • Instructors must request streaming items for each semester, identifying the specific portions needed.

Authorization is based upon course enrollment and University policy.
