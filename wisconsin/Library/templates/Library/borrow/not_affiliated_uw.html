For Those Not Affiliated with UW–Madison

Academic, Public, and Special Libraries

We provide book loans and photocopying services to libraries. Academic, public or special libraries may send ILL Lending requests , either OCLC requests (symbol "GZM") or web form requests (if OCLC is not an option for your institution). If your institution needs a web form login, please contact gzmill@library.wisc.edu . More information about our Interlibrary Loan Lending services .

Fee-based Services

The UW–Madison Libraries also offer access to our collections for a fee. This service is available to both individuals and businesses, especially those without other library affiliations. Contact Wisconsin TechSearch at (608) 262-5917 for more information.

Visitors to Campus

UW–Madison campus library visitors may borrow library materials subject to their current residence and affiliation . Use the Shelving Facility Request form to have materials from a campus shelving facility placed on hold at a campus library for viewing. More information about all services available to visitors on the Visitor Information page.

Wisconsin Residents | Non-Residents

Place your book/article requests for UW–Madison materials through your local campus library, corporate library or public library. Or, contact one of the UW–Madison fee-based document delivery services listed above.
