Borrowing Policies

In order to borrow materials from any UW–Madison library, you must present a valid UW–Madison ID, another Universities of Wisconsin campus ID, or a Community Borrowing Card.

The following table includes typical loan periods for most loans of UW–Madison materials:

Policies by Affiliation Category

Universities of Wisconsin

  • Faculty & Staff
  • Graduate Students
  • Undergraduate Students

Visitors to Campus

  • Alumni
    UW–Madison alumni and UW–Madison Alumni Association paid members.
  • Annual Fee Card Holders
    Members of the public not covered elsewhere.
  • Community Borrowing Courtesy Card Holders
    Includes Beloit College, Edgewood College, Madison College faculty, staff & students, Big Ten Academic Alliance (BTAA) faculty, staff & students, City of Madison, Dane County, and State of Wisconsin employees, PLATO members, reciprocal borrowing program participants and others.
  • Library Donors
    Donors may gain access to borrow materials with a donation of $50 or more to the Libraries' gift funds.

Patrons of Other Libraries

Patrons of other libraries make requests to borrow materials through their local library.
