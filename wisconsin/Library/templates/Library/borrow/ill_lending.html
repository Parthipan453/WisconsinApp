ILL Lending

Lending services to libraries

We provide book loans and scanning services to libraries.

  • Submit a lending request

We participate in Rapido and RapidR – please contact us if you would like to become partners. We also accept either OCLC requests to the symbol "GZM" or webform requests (if OCLC is not an option for your institution). If your institution needs a webform login, please contact our staff at gzmill@library.wisc.edu and we will gladly set you up after you provide mailing information about your library.

Lending services to individuals

Individuals who need materials from the University of Wisconsin–Madison collections should place an Interlibrary Loan request at any U.S. or overseas library to which you have access. If you are not affiliated with a library, please consult UW–Madison Libraries' fee-based services.

Lending services to businesses

Please consult UW–Madison Libraries' fee-based services.
