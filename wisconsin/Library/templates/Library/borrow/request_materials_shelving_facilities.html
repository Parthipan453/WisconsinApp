Request Materials from UW–Madison Shelving Facilities

UW–Madison Students, Staff, and Faculty

Request books and entire journal volumes via the Request a Copy link in each item's Library Catalog record. Items labeled "Must be used in-house" will be held at the selected Pickup Location for use in the library.

To request digital access to a book chapter, use the Request a Copy link in each item's Library Catalog record. Then, select Partial Digital Copy and provide details.

Visitors to UW–Madison

Visitors to campus who do not already have a Community Borrowing Card, please use this request form . Requests will take time to process. Allow three business days between the time of your request and visit. All items for visitors will be held at the selected Pickup Location for use in the library.

If you don't plan to visit campus, place your book/article requests for UW–Madison materials through your local campus library, corporate library, or public library. Or, contact Wisconsin Tech Search for paid article delivery and research services.
