About Your Borrowing History

Jump directly to the Frequently Asked Questions

Whenever an item is checked out from and returned to the library, our systems record the history of that transaction, including a reference to the person who checked out the item and which copy of the material was borrowed.

To protect your privacy, the Libraries now anonymize all borrowing history data that is older than six months. This process de-identifies the individual associated with each loan transaction. After anonymization, the Libraries retain a record of the loan for internal collection analysis purposes, but no longer connect a specific patron to that transaction.

Opting In Required to Maintain Your History

If you want to maintain your borrowing history, you will need to log in to the borrowing history service and opt in so that your history is preserved for your use.

If you decide later that you no longer want your borrowing history maintained, you can return to the borrowing history service and opt out at any time.

If you have opted in to maintain your full borrowing history, you can view your borrowing history from within your Library Account.

Exporting Your Borrowing History

Once you opt in, if you want to download your history, you can export it using the Download option in the borrowing history service. This downloads a text (.csv) file that can be stored offline or used in your own applications.

For Madison patrons, log in to your Library Account and click on BORROWING HISTORY in the upper-right corner. Your account will show one of three situations.

Situation 1 – You have not opted in

Under Borrowing History, your account indicates that borrowing history is not being saved. To opt in, choose the option to save your borrowing history.

Situation 2 – You have opted in and have borrowing history

Under Borrowing History, your account displays your borrowing history together with the option to stop saving it.

Situation 3 – You have opted in but have no borrowing history

Your account indicates that borrowing history is enabled but you have not yet accumulated any borrowing history.
