Verona Shelving Facility

The Verona Shelving Facility is closed to the public. If you would like to access materials in the Verona Shelving Facility, see our online instructions.

The Verona Shelving Facility opened and began the intake of materials in November 2014. It will eventually hold over 4 million volumes. We would like to thank those who made this facility a reality: The UW–Madison Provost, Paul DeLuca; UW–Madison Campus, UW–Madison General Library System; and donor.

Details Regarding the Facility

The Verona Shelving Facility is a dense storage facility attached to the current Materials Distribution Services (MDS) surplus with a Purpose (SWAP) facility located at 1301 Thousand Oaks Trail, Verona, WI 53593.

For more information regarding MDS/SWAP please visit the MDS/SWAP page.
