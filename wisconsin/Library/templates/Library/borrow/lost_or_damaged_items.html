Lost or Damaged Items

Lost book or damaged book fines cannot be appealed. Lost items remain the property of the University and must be returned if they are eventually found.

If you lose or damage a UW System or Interlibrary Loan book, contact Circulation Support to see if the owning library will accept a replacement copy. If not, you must pay the lost item replacement fee.

If you lose or damage a UW–Madison book, you can choose one of the following options.

  1. Pay the Replacement Fee

    • Most replacement fees are $100.00.
    • You can be reimbursed if the original item is located and returned within six months of the payment date.

  2. Purchase a Replacement Copy

    • Not all libraries accept replacement copies. Contact Circulation Support before purchasing a replacement to ensure the owning library will accept it.
    • If you choose to purchase a replacement copy, you cannot be reimbursed if the original item is later found, and the replacement copy will not be returned.
    • The replacement copy must be an exact duplicate of the original item, including the same edition, International Standard Book Number (ISBN), and publisher. Contact Circulation Support before purchasing a replacement to ensure you have the correct replacement information.
    • Library staff will not accept a paperback replacement for a hardcover original.
    • If you cannot locate an exact duplicate, contact Circulation Support . Library staff will determine whether an alternate edition can be accepted.

Questions? Contact the UW–Madison Libraries Circulation Support for assistance with lost or damaged library materials.
