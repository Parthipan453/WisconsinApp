How transfer works

Transferring a user closes their current allocation (marked Completed, with a move-out date of the transfer date) and opens a fresh Active allocation in the new room.

The old room's occupancy is freed up immediately, and the same conflict checks (room full, gender/hostel eligibility) apply to the new room.
